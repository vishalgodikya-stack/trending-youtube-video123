Frequently Asked Questions

TrendWave is a real-time, distraction-free dashboard designed to discover what the world is actually watching on YouTube today. It cuts through algorithm clutter to surface genuine viral topics and top-performing releases across global and regional markets.

We enforce strict quality standards: videos must be uploaded within the last 24 hours (present day), have a minimum of 5,000 views, and cannot be active livestreams. Qualified videos are ranked strictly from highest to lowest view count so you see the biggest hits first.

Regional Mode tracks localized trends in countries like India, the US, or the UK. Global Mode eliminates regional television broadcasts, soap operas, and local scripts to aggregate universally recognized international hits (like Billboard singles, movie trailers, and global creator events) with a higher 50K+ view floor.

Clicking any video card searches YouTube directly for that specific trending topic, allowing you to see multiple creator perspectives, analysis, and reactions instead of getting trapped in a single video's sidebar recommendations. To watch the video directly, simply click the dedicated Watch button on the card!
