Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy outlines how TrendWave handles data, respects your privacy, and adheres to global privacy regulations and advertising standards.

Effective Date: September 2026 5 min read

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to TrendWave (accessible at https://trendwaveyoutube.vercel.app/). TrendWave provides a real-time, algorithmic-free discovery tool that aggregates and visualizes publicly available trending YouTube video metadata.

We are dedicated to safeguarding the personal data and privacy of everyone who visits our platform. This Privacy Policy clarifies what information is processed when you visit our website, how third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) utilize cookies, and the controls available to you.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

TrendWave operates on a privacy-first foundation:

  • No User Accounts: We do not require account creation, registration, usernames, or passwords.
  • No Personal Profile Tracking: We do not build personal browsing profiles, purchase histories, or cross-device identity records.
  • No Financial Details: TrendWave is free to use and never requests credit card or banking information.

3. Log Files & Automated Technical Data

Like virtually all modern web applications, TrendWave uses standard hosting log files provided by our cloud hosting infrastructure (such as Vercel). The information collected by log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized/truncated where applicable)
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamps
  • Referring and exit pages

This technical data is not linked to any personally identifiable information. It is strictly used for analyzing server performance, monitoring bandwidth, preventing malicious DDoS activity, and improving platform responsiveness.

4. Cookies, Web Beacons & Google AdSense

TrendWave may partner with third-party advertising networks, specifically Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our site.

Google AdSense Cookie Disclosure:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted ads to our users based on their visits to TrendWave and/or other websites across the web.

How to Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising

You have full control over your advertising preferences:

  • You may opt out of Google personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative or www.aboutads.info.
  • You can also instruct your web browser to reject all cookies or notify you when a cookie is being sent via your browser settings.

5. YouTube API Services & Third-Party Embeds

TrendWave displays publicly accessible video titles, channel names, view statistics, and embeddable video streams sourced through public YouTube data interfaces and privacy-friendly mirrors.

By engaging with video playback on TrendWave, you may interact with YouTube's embedded media infrastructure. Consequently, your interactions are also subject to:

  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

TrendWave does not access your private YouTube account, subscription list, or watch history.

6. Local Storage Preferences

We use your browser's client-side localStorage exclusively to enhance your browsing experience:

  • Theme Preference: Remembers whether you selected Dark Mode or Light Mode so your choice persists between visits.
  • Region Preference: Remembers your chosen country filter (e.g., India, US, UK, Global).

This information remains entirely within your local device and is never transmitted to our servers.

7. GDPR Rights (For European Union Users)

If you reside within the European Economic Area (EEA), you possess specific data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • The right to access: Request copies of any personal data held about you.
  • The right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • The right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict or object to processing: Limit how third-party ad vendors process your data.

Because TrendWave does not store personal profiles, we do not maintain identifiable user files. For inquiries, please reach out via our contact channels.

8. CCPA / CPRA Rights (For California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California consumers are entitled to:

  • Request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal information collected.
  • Request deletion of personal information collected.
  • Direct a business that sells or shares consumer personal information not to sell or share it. TrendWave does NOT sell personal information.
  • Exercise these rights without receiving discriminatory treatment.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting children's online privacy is paramount. TrendWave is a general-audience website and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has provided personal details on our site, please contact us immediately so we can promptly take appropriate corrective action.

10. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions, clarifications, or requests concerning this Privacy Policy or our data handling practices, please contact us:

  • Online Form: Visit our Contact Page
  • Website: https://trendwaveyoutube.vercel.app/
