Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how TrendWave discovers, filters, and ranks real-time YouTube trending content.

1 What is TrendWave?

TrendWave is a lightweight, distraction-free dashboard built to discover what the world is actually watching on YouTube right now. Instead of endless recommendations and algorithmic bias, TrendWave gives you a clean view of the highest-velocity trending topics, songs, trailers, and viral moments across global and regional markets.

2 How are trending videos ranked and filtered?

We enforce strict quality and freshness standards before any video reaches the grid:

• Strict 24-Hour Cutoff: Only videos uploaded within the current day (hours or minutes ago) are eligible.
• 5,000+ Minimum Views: Low-engagement uploads and spam are eliminated.
• No Live Streams: Ongoing broadcasts with small live viewer counts are filtered out in favor of completed, high-view viral uploads.
• Ranked High to Low: Qualified videos are sorted strictly from highest to lowest view count, putting the biggest viral hits first.

3 What is the difference between Regional and Global mode?

Regional Mode tracks localized trends within specific countries (India, United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Japan, Germany, and Brazil).

Global Mode filters out regional television networks, daily serials, and vernacular language scripts (Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Bengali, etc.) to aggregate universally recognized international phenomena — like worldwide Billboard music, blockbuster Hollywood movie trailers, and global creator milestones — with a 50,000+ view floor.

4 Why does clicking a card explore the topic on YouTube?

When you click any video card, TrendWave searches YouTube directly for that specific trending topic. This allows you to explore diverse creator perspectives, journalistic coverage, and reactions instead of getting trapped in a single video's sidebar recommendations.

If you simply want to watch that specific video directly, click the dedicated Watch button in the bottom-right corner of any card.

5 Is any personal login or account required?

No. TrendWave is 100% open and privacy-friendly. No Google login, tracking cookies, or analytics profiles are used. Your dark/light theme and country preferences are saved purely inside your own browser using localStorage.
